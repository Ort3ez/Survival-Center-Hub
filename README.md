# Survival Center Hub
The purpose of this product or feature is to provide people in the community with an application that allows them to receive updates about the schedule for the Northampton/Amherst Survival center, different available bus routes to get there, urgent volunteer requests and notifications about when the Mobile Food Pantry is available(usually on the first Wednesdays of every month from 1:00PM to 2:00PM). This product gives people a central application where they can access a variety of data that they would otherwise have to get provided to them by others or do extensive research to get. Although this application is mainly to assist people who need these services, it also gives people who help the opportunity to volunteer by letting them know when they are able to.
